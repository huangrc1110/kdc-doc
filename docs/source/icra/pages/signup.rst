******************************
Registration
******************************

This competition comprises of teams of 1–4 people. Please sign-up for the competition first before forming teams, and all members within all teams are required to be on the signup list and have their identities verified. There are no costs for signing up for this competition. Please thoroughly read the full rules and requirements laid out on the competition website prior to signing up.

Signing Up
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

The sign-up process is simple and intuitive. Follow these easy steps for the competition sign-up:

- Open our competition's official website using this link provided `here <https://www.kdc.icra.lejurobot.com/home>`_
- Click the "Start Now" button as shown on the main page, and simply fill in your personal information as prompted to sign up for this competition

Logging In
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

- Open our competition's official website using this link provided `here <https://www.kdc.icra.lejurobot.com/home>`_
- Locate the profile icon on the top right corner of the website, click on that, and log in using your credentials as from the previous section.

Vienna On-Site Registration
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

- Teams that plan to participate in the Vienna on-site competition must complete the ICRA 2026 Competition-only registration before 2026/05/25.
- The Competition-only registration should be completed using the official ICRA registration site:
  `ICRA 2026 Registration Information <https://2026.ieee-icra.org/attend/registration-information/>`_
  and `ICRA 2026 Registration Site <https://aimgroup.eventsair.com/icra2026-regi/icra2026-registration/Site/Register>`_.
- If you have already submitted a real-robot track model, you may continue to use the same account for on-site competition preparation and submission.
- The registration coupon code for the Competition-only track can be obtained from the REAL-I 2026 staff.

Note
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

- After registration, you will be in a team where you are the team leader. You can joint other teams or let others join your team. Each person has 3 chances to join a team.
- All submission and ranking will be based on the team level. Every team member is able to submit submissions for the competition.
- Vienna on-site participation requires both team registration and completion of the Competition-only registration step.
